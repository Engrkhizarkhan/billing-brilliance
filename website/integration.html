What the Connection Does

Four Things FinTap
Does for Your System

Create a Payment Request

Your system sends us a person's name and the amount due. FinTap creates a payment voucher they can use at any bank to pay.

Check Payment Status

At any time, your system can ask FinTap whether a specific payment has been made. You get a clear Paid / Unpaid answer.

Receive Payment Confirmation

The moment a payment goes through at any bank, FinTap automatically notifies your system. No manual checking needed.

Track Payment History

Your system can pull a list of all payments at any time, including who paid, how much, when, and through which bank.

Built on Pakistan's National Standard

Connected Through
Pakistan's National Payment Network

All connections are fully encrypted. Data cannot be intercepted in transit
Every payment confirmation is verified before being recorded
Only approved banking network servers can send payment data
Each payment has a unique reference that cannot be duplicated
The same payment cannot be recorded twice, even if the network sends it twice
Full sandbox testing completed before any live payments are processed
1LINK National Payment Network

FinTap is built on 1LINK, Pakistan's national interbank payment network. Payments made through FinTap vouchers are processed through the same infrastructure used by major banks, ATM networks, and mobile banking apps across Pakistan.
