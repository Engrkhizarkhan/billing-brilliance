Simple Process

It's as Simple as
Three Steps.

No complicated setup. No teaching parents a new app. They already have a bank account — that's all they need.

STEP 01

Your institution creates a bill

Your admin enters the fee details — student name, amount, due date. Payniva generates a unique payment voucher automatically.

STEP 02

The person pays from any bank

Parents or applicants pay using internet banking, a mobile app, ATM, or over the counter at any bank branch — whichever is easiest.

STEP 03

Your dashboard updates instantly

The moment payment is made, your portal marks it as paid. No manual entries, no phone calls to confirm, no delays.
