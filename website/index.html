COMMON QUESTIONS

A few practical details.

Talk to our team ↗
Do parents or customers need a FinTap account?+

No. They pay using their payment reference through a supported banking channel. Your staff use FinTap to manage bills and payment records.

Which banks can people pay through?+

FinTap is built around the 1LINK / 1BILL network, so people can pay through participating banks across Pakistan. We’ll confirm the available channels and biller setup with you during onboarding.

Can we connect our existing software?+

Yes. Organizations can connect their systems to create payment requests and receive payment updates. Our team can walk through your requirements with you.

How do we discuss pricing?+

Contact our team with your payment volume and requirements. We’ll discuss the commercial terms with you before you get started.
