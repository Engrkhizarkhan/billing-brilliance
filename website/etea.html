Important — Ownership Boundary

Payniva is your payment processor, not your applicant database.

Applicant master data ownership remains with your source system (ETEA). Payniva stores temporary payment records scoped to each application — it processes payment create, status, and callback, then notifies your source system. It does not become the system of record for applicant profiles.

Workflow

The ETEA Payment Lifecycle

From posting creation to source-system notification — every step is traceable and idempotent.

01
Posting Setup

Create an Exam Posting

Define the posting with fee amount, eligible applicants range, and payment deadline. The posting generates a unique biller reference registered with 1BILL.

02
Payment Request

Applicant Payment Created

Your source system calls POST /api/payments/create with the application ID and amount. Payniva generates a PSID and returns it to your source system.

03
Payment Execution

Applicant Pays via Bank Channel

The applicant uses their PSID at any 1LINK-connected bank: ATM, internet banking, mobile app, or OTC agent. No app download or Payniva account required.

04
Callback Processing

1BILL Sends Payment Callback

1BILL POSTs a signed callback to Payniva. The signature is validated using HMAC, the transaction ID is checked for idempotency, and the payment record is updated.

05
Status Notification

Source System Notified

Payniva calls POST /api/etea/payment-status to notify your upstream system of the final payment status — CONFIRMED or FAILED — so you can proceed with applicant processing.

Five Canonical ETEA Endpoints

Clean, predictable REST. Designed for direct integration from your source system.

# Receive callback from 1BILL after payment
POST  /api/payment/callback

# Notify your source system of payment status
POST  /api/etea/payment-status

# Health check for monitoring
GET   /api/health
