The Connection

How Your System Connects
to FinTap.

You don't need to rebuild your software. FinTap sits between your existing system and Pakistan's banking network.

01

Your system creates a payment request

When an applicant, patient, or student needs to pay, your system tells FinTap: "This person owes PKR X." FinTap creates a unique payment voucher.

02

The person pays at any bank

They take the voucher number to any bank in person, online, or at an ATM. They enter the number, see the amount, and pay. No new app, no new account needed.

03

FinTap tells your system instantly

The moment the bank confirms the payment, FinTap automatically notifies your system. Your system can then unlock the service, confirm the application, or update its records.

04

Everything is tracked and reportable

Every payment is logged, whether successful, pending, or failed, in the Organization Portal. You can search, filter, and export reports at any time.

Common Questions

Questions Organizations Ask.

Do we need to rebuild our existing software? →
No. FinTap is designed to work alongside your existing software. Your IT team adds a connection to FinTap. The rest of your system stays exactly as it is.
How long does integration take? →
Most organizations complete the technical integration in one to two weeks. We provide a sandbox environment so your team can test everything before going live.
Do applicants or customers need a special account? →
No. They only need a standard bank account, which most people already have. They pay using internet banking, a mobile app, ATM, or bank branch, exactly how they pay utility bills today.
Is our applicant/customer data shared with FinTap? →
FinTap only receives what is needed to create a payment request, typically an ID and an amount. We do not store personal records, application data, or business logic. Your data remains in your system.
What happens if a payment fails or is reversed? →
Failed and reversed payments are tracked and your system is notified automatically. You can also view all transaction statuses (successful, pending, or failed) in the Organization Portal at any time.
