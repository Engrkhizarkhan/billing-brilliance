Your Data

Your Data is Always Yours.
Nobody Else Can See It.

Every school and organization on FinTap has a completely separate space. There is no way for one organization to see another's data, including accidental access.

School A users can never see School B's students or payments
Each organization's records are stored and accessed separately
Every login is verified before accessing any data
Reports and exports only show your organization's own data
All data is encrypted in storage and in transit
Access is controlled by role. Staff only see what they need
