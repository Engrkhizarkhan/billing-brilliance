How It Works

From Posting a Fee to
Receiving the Money.

Here's exactly what happens when you use Payniva for your school's fee collection.

01

Your admin posts the fee bill

A staff member logs into the School Portal, selects the student or class, enters the fee amount and due date, and saves it. That's the whole process on your end.

02

Payniva generates a payment voucher

A unique voucher number is created for each fee bill. This number is what the parent uses when they go to pay — no app, no login, just their bank.

03

The parent pays at any bank

The parent opens their bank app, visits an ATM, or walks into any bank branch. They enter the voucher number, confirm the amount, and pay. Done — in under two minutes.

04

Your portal updates in real time

The moment the bank confirms the payment, your School Portal marks the fee as paid. You don't need to check with anyone or enter anything manually.

05

Reports are always ready

At any time — daily, weekly, monthly — you can see who has paid, who hasn't, and how much has been collected in total. Export to a spreadsheet in one click.

Common Questions

Questions Schools Ask
Before Getting Started.

Do parents need to download an app? →
No. Parents use their existing bank app, internet banking, ATM, or a bank branch counter. There's nothing new to install or register for.
How long does it take to get set up? →
Most schools are up and running within a few days. Our team handles the registration on the payment network and walks your finance staff through the portal.
What if a parent pays the wrong amount? →
The payment voucher is tied to a specific amount. The bank will only accept the exact amount listed on the bill, so overpayment and underpayment are prevented automatically.
Can we use this for a school network with multiple branches? →
Yes. Each branch is set up as its own school in the system. All data stays separate between branches, but the managing organization can see everything from one place.
Is the money deposited directly into our school's account? →
Yes. Payments go through the national payment network and are settled into your school's designated bank account according to the settlement schedule.
