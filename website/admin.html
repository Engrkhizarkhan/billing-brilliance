Admin Capabilities

What the Platform Admin Controls

Biller Lifecycle Management

Create, update, suspend, or ban billers. Unique biller codes are auto-generated and linked to their 1BILL registration.

User & Role Management

Create users with specific roles and optional tenant references. Ban, unban, and manage email verification states.

Platform Transaction Monitor

Search, filter, and inspect every transaction across all tenants by status, amount, date, and payment channel.

Platform-Wide Audit Log

Every mutation logged with timestamp, actor, action type, tenant, and IP address. Immutable and exportable.

API Sandbox

Test BillInquiry, BillPayment, and callback flows against the 1LINK specification without touching production data.

Real-Time Payment Visibility

Live feed of incoming payment callbacks across all tenants with status, channel, amount, and tenant context.
